Sipsik Love Privacy

Short and human: what we store and how to delete it. Updated November 9, 2026.

Only the two of you see your data. We don’t sell it, don’t show it to advertisers and don’t use it for anything except running the app.

What we store

  • From Telegram: your ID, name and username. Telegram passes them when you open the app.
  • What you add yourself: your relationship start date, moods, notes, wishes with photos, shopping, chores, plans, loved ones’ birthdays, important dates, places, answers to daily questions and memories with photos.
  • Service data: when you last opened the app and how many times you tapped the cat. This is needed for streaks, reminders and weekly summaries.
  • Purchases with Telegram Stars: what was bought and the payment ID. This is needed for support and refunds. We never see card details.
  • The language you chose in the app.

Who sees it

Only you and your partner. Other couples see nothing. Two exceptions inside a couple: the owner of a wish doesn’t see who is preparing the gift, and the sender of a note doesn’t know when it will arrive.

Where it’s stored

In a Supabase database in Europe (London). Photos are kept in private storage and open only through temporary links inside the app.

Messages from the bot

The bot writes when your partner marks something, when a note arrives, about birthdays, important dates, weekly summaries and occasional notes from Sipsik. Notifications about your partner’s actions can be turned off in the app settings.

How to delete your data

  • Leave the couple: app settings → “Leave the couple”. Your wishes are deleted. If you were alone in the couple, the whole couple is deleted.
  • Delete your whole account: send /deleteme to the bot. Your name, moods, notes, your wishes with photos and everything linked only to you will be deleted. The couple’s shared lists and memories stay with your partner.

Questions

Write to the bot @SipsikLoveBot or to the app’s creator on Telegram.
